# Public website art direction

The homepage leads with the original Queen of Quiapo wordmark, the 2027 theme, and the application action. The programme follows immediately. The transparent Q emblem has its own column in the community story. The remaining light section explains how candidates prepare. Public pages share the same navigation and footer.

The new background is decorative artwork, not documentary imagery. It is positioned to leave the wordmark and theme readable. No candidate, winner, crown, venue or event photograph is invented. The existing wordmark and transparent emblem are preserved.

## Background asset

- File: `public/assets/pageant-atmosphere.webp`
- Mode: new image generation with the built-in image-generation tool, opaque background
- Web delivery: WebP quality 88; no creative alterations during conversion

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: a wide cinematic background plate for the Queen of Quiapo 2027 pageant website. Create a sophisticated midnight-navy galaxy atmosphere, designed by a fashion campaign art director. Deep nearly black indigo open space across the middle left and middle right so a metallic pageant wordmark and elegant white typography can be placed there separately in HTML. Along the FAR RIGHT and LOWER edge only, one graceful flowing ribbon of luminous lavender nebula dust and thin pearlescent silver light, like the train of a couture gown traced through space; real fine starlight, extremely sparse and controlled. A subtle distant galaxy glow confined to the upper-right corner. Refined, photographic tonal depth, restrained contrast, silver and soft violet highlights, no magenta neon, no orange, no crowded star field. Wide landscape 16:9 composition, high resolution. The lower center fades to nearly solid midnight navy for a slim information strip. Produce a single edge-to-edge background image, not a website screenshot. Absolutely no people, models, faces, crowns, sashes, logos, words, numbers, frames, panels or UI. The art must stay at the edges; generous dark negative space is essential. Avoid giant spiral galaxy disks or an oval cloud centered behind the logo. Think elegant evening coronation campaign, with quiet celestial detailing and no generic sci-fi planets.

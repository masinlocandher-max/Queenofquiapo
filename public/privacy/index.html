Privacy Notice

What we collect, why, who can see it, and how to ask us to remove it.

Who we are

Queen of Quiapo 2027 is a community pageant in Barangay 306, Quiapo, Manila. This website is managed for the organizers by FMB&CO. The organizers decide how the personal information described here is used.

If you apply as a candidate

The application form collects your legal name, pageant name, email address, mobile number (optional), the city or municipality you represent, your height and measurements, your optional answers about talent, experience and motivation, and two photographs: a close-up and a full-body swimsuit photograph.

We use this only to screen applicants for Queen of Quiapo 2027 and to contact you about your application. We rely on the consent you give when you submit the form.

  • Only authorized organizers can open applications. They are not visible to judges, to other applicants or to the public.
  • Photographs are kept in private storage and are shown only to an authorized organizer who is signed in.
  • Your reference number does not unlock your application. Nobody can view your details by knowing it.
  • Screening decisions are not published.

If you become an official candidate

Only your approved pageant name, candidate number, an official portrait and an optional short biography are shown publicly, and only after the organizers have recorded your separate permission for publicity. Your screening photographs, measurements and contact details are never copied to the public site.

If you support a candidate with voting points

Online payment has not opened. When it does, we will keep your email address (for your receipt), your optional name, the candidate and package you chose, the amount, and the payment reference from the payment provider. Card and e-wallet details are entered with the payment provider, not on this website, and we do not receive or store them. This notice will be updated with the provider’s name before any payment is accepted.

Judges and organizers

We keep the name and sign-in email of each judge and organizer, the scores a judge submits, and a record of significant actions (for example opening judging or certifying results) with the time and the account that took them. This protects the integrity of the results.

Technical information

To limit abuse, the site briefly counts requests using a one-way scrambled form of your network address. It cannot be used to identify you and is deleted regularly. The site does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

How long we keep it

Applications that do not proceed are removed after the edition ends, and at any time on request. Records needed to verify official results and payments are kept as long as the organizers are required to account for them.

Your choices

You can withdraw your application from your receipt page while it is open. At any time you may ask the organizers to show you, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent. Requests can be made to the organizers at Barangay 306, Quiapo, Manila, or through the official channels announced on this website. You also have the right to raise a concern with the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change, including before online payment opens. This notice was last updated in November 2026.

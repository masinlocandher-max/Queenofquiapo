Terms of Use

Plain terms for visitors, applicants and supporters.

About this website

This is the official website of Queen of Quiapo 2027. It provides pageant information, candidate applications, the official candidate roster, voting information and, once released, official results.

Official information

Dates, venues and announcements are official only as shown on this website. Items marked “to be announced” have not been confirmed. The schedule may change; changes are posted here.

Applications

Give accurate information and use only photographs of yourself that you have the right to submit. Submitting an application does not guarantee selection. The organizers’ screening decisions are final. Applications that are false, abusive or submitted for someone else without permission may be removed.

Voting points

Voting works as described in the Voting Rules. Online payment is not yet available. Do not send money to any person or account claiming to sell Queen of Quiapo voting points: purchases will be possible only through the checkout on this website once the organizers announce that it is open.

Results

Only results published on the Results page after certification by the organizers are official.

Fair use

Do not attempt to disrupt the site, access private areas, interfere with applications, scoring or voting, or submit automated requests. We may block access that puts the event or other people’s information at risk.

Your information

Our Privacy Notice explains what we collect and your choices.

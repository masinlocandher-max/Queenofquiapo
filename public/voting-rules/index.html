Voting Rules

How voting points work. Online payment has not opened, and some rules are still to be confirmed by the organizers.

The rate

₱100 = 1,000 voting points. That is ₱1 for 10 points. The rate is the same for every package. There are no discounts, bonus points or multipliers.

The packages on the voting page are the current list. If the organizers revise a package before voting opens, that page is updated.

How a purchase works

  1. Choose one official candidate.
  2. Choose a package.
  3. Review your selection and pay through the payment provider.
  4. Your points are added to your candidate once the payment provider confirms the payment to us. A “payment successful” screen in your browser is not that confirmation; the confirmed status appears on your order.

You may buy as many packages as you like, as many times as you like, for any candidate. Each purchase is counted once.

Your points stay with your candidate

You choose the candidate before paying. The points from that purchase belong to that candidate and cannot be moved to another candidate afterwards.

How totals are counted

A candidate’s total is the sum of points from confirmed payments, less any payment that is later refunded or reversed. Totals are kept in a permanent record that the organizers reconcile against the payment provider’s reports before the final count.

Voting points and the judges

Voting points and the judges’ scores are counted separately. Judges score candidates on the official criteria, and those scores alone produce the judges’ ranking. Buying points does not change a judge’s score.

Still to be announced

The organizers will publish the following here before voting opens. Until then, no payment is accepted.

  • The dates and times voting opens and closes (Philippine time).
  • The accepted payment methods and the payment provider.
  • The refund and cancellation policy.
  • What happens to a payment started before the deadline but confirmed after it.
  • What the voting points decide, including the name of any award.

Questions

For questions about voting, contact the organizers at Barangay 306, Quiapo, Manila, or through the official channels announced on this website.

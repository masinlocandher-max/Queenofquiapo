THE PAGEANT & ITS PEOPLE

About Queen of Quiapo

During the feast of Poong Nazareno, Quiapo’s LGBTQ+ community takes its place onstage.

A Tribute to Faith, Tradition, and Grace.

For some candidates, joining Queen of Quiapo is a panata, a personal vow to Poong Nazareno. Yeng Lavitoria described her return to the pageant as part of that vow. Fellow candidate Patricia Payumo spoke of the sisterhood she found among the other queens.

How the community remembers it

Some in the community trace the pageant’s beginnings to 2012. Others remember it from earlier years. The date of the first coronation remains an open question.

By January 2016, a pageant report was already describing Miss Gay Quiapo as a yearly competition. It named Alexis Romualdez-Centino as that year’s winner.

Sources for the pageant’s history
  • Trans Pageantry Philippines, January 7, 2016 listed the winners of that year’s Miss Gay Quiapo.
  • PhilSTAR L!fe, January 8, 2024 interviewed candidates about their panata and the friendships they built through the pageant.
